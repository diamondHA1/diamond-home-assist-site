Services

Everyday help at home

Hands-on, non-medical care for seniors and adults with disabilities through Michigan's Medicaid Home Help program, from a trained Diamond caregiver or a family member we hire, so they can stay safely in their own home.

What Home Help covers

Help with daily tasks

Personal care

Bathing, grooming, dressing, using the toilet, and eating.

Moving around safely

Help getting in and out of bed or a chair, and walking around the home.

Help with medications

Hands-on help taking medications as prescribed.

Meal preparation

Planning and cooking meals, with their dietary needs in mind.

Housework & laundry

Light housekeeping, dishes, laundry, and fresh linens.

Shopping

Grocery and pharmacy shopping for the things they need.

Good to know: Home Help pays for hands-on tasks the state approves for each person. It does not pay for supervision, reminders or companionship on their own, transportation, or heavy chores like yard work and home repairs. Exact hours and tasks are set by MDHHS after an in-home assessment.
Who provides the care

Our caregiver, or yours

We provide the caregiver

A trained Diamond caregiver

We match each client with a background-checked, trained caregiver who lives nearby and fits their schedule and language.

Someone you already trust

A family member or friend

An adult child, sibling, other relative or friend can be hired by Diamond and paid for the approved hours. Spouses and parents of minor children can't be paid under Home Help.

How care is paid

Most families pay nothing

Michigan Medicaid Home Help

Home Help is paid by Michigan Medicaid, so there's no cost to the family. Our help with the application is free too.

Have Medicare and Medicaid?

If your loved one is in a Medicaid health plan or an MI Coordinated Health plan, their personal care may come through that plan instead. Call us and we'll help you sort it out.
