Services

Everyday help at home

Hands-on, non-medical care through Michigan's Medicaid Home Help program — so your loved one can stay safely in their own home.

What Home Help covers

Help with daily tasks

🛁

Personal care

Bathing, grooming, dressing, using the toilet, and eating.

🦯

Moving around safely

Help getting in and out of bed or a chair, and walking around the home.

💊

Help with medications

Hands-on help taking medications as prescribed.

🍲

Meal preparation

Planning and cooking meals, with their dietary needs in mind.

🧺

Housework & laundry

Light housekeeping, dishes, laundry, and fresh linens.

🛒

Shopping

Grocery and pharmacy shopping for the things they need.

Good to know: Home Help pays for hands-on tasks the state approves for your loved one. It does not pay for supervision, reminders or companionship on their own, transportation, or heavy chores like yard work and home repairs. Exact hours and tasks are set by MDHHS after an in-home assessment.
