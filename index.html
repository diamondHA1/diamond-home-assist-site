Who qualifies

Your family may qualify if

  • Your loved one has Michigan Medicaid
  • They need hands-on help with bathing, dressing, eating, or moving around
  • They live at home in Detroit or Wayne County
  • The caregiver is an adult child, sibling, grandchild, other relative, or friend

Under Home Help rules, a spouse or the parent of a minor child can't be the paid caregiver. MDHHS makes the final eligibility decision.

Questions

What families ask us

Does it cost my family anything?

No. Home Help is paid by Michigan Medicaid, and our help with the application is free.

Can I really get paid to care for my mom or dad?

Often, yes. Adult children, siblings, other relatives and friends can be paid caregivers through Home Help. Spouses cannot. You'd work as a Diamond Home Assist caregiver and be paid for the hours MDHHS approves.

What if they don't have Medicaid yet?

Take the eligibility check anyway and choose "I'm not sure." We'll point you in the right direction.

How long does it take?

It depends on when MDHHS schedules the in-home assessment. We submit everything quickly and follow up so the case keeps moving.

What if no one in the family can provide care?

We'll match your loved one with one of our trained Diamond caregivers.
