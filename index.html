Who qualifies

They may qualify if

  • They have Michigan Medicaid
  • They need hands-on help with bathing, dressing, eating, or moving around
  • They live in a private home in Detroit or Wayne County
  • Care can come from a Diamond caregiver, or an adult relative or friend

Under Home Help rules, a spouse or the parent of a minor child can't be the paid caregiver. Home Help doesn't cover care in a nursing home, assisted living or adult foster care home. MDHHS makes the final eligibility decision.

Questions

What people ask us

Getting care

Does it cost my family anything?

No. Home Help is paid by Michigan Medicaid, and our help with the application is free.

Do we have to use a family member as the caregiver?

No. If no one in the family can provide care, or you'd rather not, we match your loved one with one of our trained Diamond caregivers.

Can I get paid to care for my mom or dad?

Often, yes. Adult children, siblings, other relatives and friends can be paid caregivers through Home Help. Spouses cannot. You'd work as a Diamond Home Assist caregiver and be paid for the hours MDHHS approves.

What if they don't have Medicaid yet?

Take the eligibility check anyway and choose "I'm not sure." We'll point you in the right direction.

How long does it take?

It depends on when MDHHS schedules the in-home assessment. We submit everything quickly and follow up so the case keeps moving.

Working as a caregiver

Do I need a license or certification?

No license is required for Home Help personal care. CNA, home health aide or caregiving experience is a plus, and we provide training.

What does a Diamond caregiver do?

Hands-on help at the client's home: bathing, dressing, grooming, meals, light housework, laundry, shopping, and help with medications as prescribed.

Where will I work?

In clients' homes across Detroit and Wayne County. We match you with clients close to where you live.

How do I clock in?

On your smartphone. Michigan requires electronic visit verification (EVV), and we show you how it works.

I already care for a relative. Can I apply?

Yes. If they have Medicaid and need hands-on help, you may be able to get paid through Home Help. Choose "I care for a family member" on the application.
