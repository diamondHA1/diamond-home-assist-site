3

Once approved, you're hired as a Diamond caregiver and paid for authorized hours. No family member available? We'll match a trained caregiver.

Who qualifies

You may qualify if…

  • Your loved one has Michigan Medicaid
  • They need help with daily tasks like bathing, dressing, eating, or getting around
  • They live in Detroit or Wayne County
  • The caregiver is an adult child, sibling, grandchild, other relative, or friend*

*Under Home Help rules, spouses and parents of minor children can't be paid caregivers. Final eligibility is decided by MDHHS.

Questions

Common questions

Does it cost my family anything?

No. Home Help is paid by Michigan Medicaid, and our help with the application is free.

Can I really get paid to care for my mom or dad?

Often, yes. Adult children, siblings, other relatives and friends can be paid caregivers through Home Help. Spouses cannot. You'd work as a Diamond Home Assist caregiver and be paid for the hours MDHHS approves.

What if they don't have Medicaid yet?

Fill out the form anyway and choose "Not sure." We'll point you in the right direction.

How long does it take?

It depends on MDHHS scheduling the in-home assessment. We submit everything quickly and follow up so your case keeps moving.

What if no family member can provide care?

We'll match your loved one with one of our trained Diamond caregivers.
