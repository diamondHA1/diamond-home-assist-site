The job

What you'll do

  • Help with bathing, dressing and grooming
  • Help clients move safely around their home
  • Prepare meals and do light housekeeping and laundry
  • Run errands and give hands-on help with medications
  • Be a steady, kind presence

Requirements

  • 18+ and authorized to work in the U.S.
  • Pass a background check
  • Smartphone to clock in/out (EVV)
  • Reliable and caring — experience a plus, not required
