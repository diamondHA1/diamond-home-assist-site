About us

Care worth its weight in diamonds

We help Michigan families care for the people they love — at home, with dignity, and without the paperwork headache.

Our story

Built for families, not paperwork

Most seniors want to stay in their own homes. Most families already do the caregiving — often unpaid, and often confused by the state system.

Diamond Home Assist is an MDHHS-approved Home Help agency in Detroit. We walk families through every step, hire family members as paid caregivers when the rules allow, and provide trained caregivers when they don't.
