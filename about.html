About us

Care worth its weight in diamonds

We help Michigan seniors and adults with disabilities get the care they need at home, and we give the caregivers who provide it steady, respected work.

Our story

Built for people, not paperwork

Most seniors want to stay in their own homes. Some have family nearby who already help every day. Many don't, and the state system can be confusing either way.

Diamond Home Assist is an MDHHS-approved Home Help agency in Detroit. We match clients with trained caregivers, hire family members as paid caregivers when the rules allow, and walk everyone through every step of the process.

Who we serve

Two groups of people, one mission

People who need care

Seniors and adults with disabilities on Michigan Medicaid who need hands-on help to stay safe at home, and the families who love them.

The caregivers who provide it

Experienced aides, people new to caregiving, and family members caring for someone they love. Every one of them deserves training, support and a team that answers.
