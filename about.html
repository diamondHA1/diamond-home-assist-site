Our story

Diamond Home Assist serves Michigan families with non-medical, in-home personal care.

Too many families struggle to find help they can trust. Too many seniors only want to stay in their own homes. We built an agency around dependable caregivers and families who deserve clarity — not confusion — from the state system.
